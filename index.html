How to Play

The Mission

Roll a legendary all-time NBA dynasty — then draft a squad good enough to beat them statistically.

The Draft

Each round, spin to land on a real franchise & decade.

You'll see every player who actually suited up for that team in that decade — scroll the list.

Pick one, then assign them to any open position.

Salary Cap

Every player has a cost based on their stats. You've got a budget — you can't just grab the 5 best. Make tradeoffs.

Game Mode

Classic — any dynasty, even matchup. Real GM — toughest dynasties, tighter cap, and they play 8% harder.

Winning

Fill all 5 spots: PG SG SF PF C

Your five and theirs become a simulated final score from all five stats — outscore the dynasty to win.
